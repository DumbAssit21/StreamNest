# StreamNest Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 16, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 19, 2026  
**Publisher:** Obsidian Interactive  
**Application:** StreamNest

## 1. Overview

StreamNest is a Windows desktop application that allows users to browse movies and television shows, access supported streaming services, and coordinate viewing sessions with friends.

Obsidian Interactive respects user privacy and aims to limit the collection and processing of personal information.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Computer

StreamNest stores certain information locally to provide its features, including:

- Selected streaming services and application preferences.
- Saved movies and television shows in your watchlist.
- Browser cookies and session information from streaming providers to maintain sign-ins.
- An optional personal TMDB API credential when that configuration is used.
- Watch Party preferences and related settings, where applicable.

This information is stored within the application's local data and browser-session storage.

## 3. Streaming Service Accounts

StreamNest opens official streaming service websites, including Netflix, Hulu, Disney+, HBO Max, and Crunchyroll.

Users sign in directly through those providers' websites. StreamNest does not operate its own streaming-account login service or transmit streaming passwords to Obsidian Interactive's catalog backend.

Streaming providers may collect information under their own privacy policies, including account activity, viewing history, and cookies.

## 4. Catalog Searches and Network Requests

StreamNest uses a shared catalog service hosted on Cloudflare Workers to retrieve movie and television information from The Movie Database (TMDB).

Search terms, catalog filters, and related network request information may be processed by Cloudflare and TMDB. Cloudflare may also process technical information such as IP addresses.

Catalog responses may be temporarily cached. StreamNest does not require a separate user account for accessing the shared catalog.

## 5. Watch Party

StreamNest provides an optional Watch Party feature that allows users to coordinate viewing sessions with friends.

When using Watch Party, information sent to StreamNest's Cloudflare-hosted room service may include:

- Watch Party room codes and room identifiers.
- Participant display names.
- Selected movies, television shows, or episodes.
- Playback positions and synchronization timestamps.
- Playback commands, including play, pause, and seek.
- Information needed to coordinate room membership and connected participants.

This information is processed to create rooms, allow participants to join, and coordinate playback.

Other participants in the same Watch Party may be able to view participant names, selected titles, and synchronization status.

Cloudflare may process technical network information, including IP addresses, in connection with operating the service.

### Streaming Accounts and Video Content

Each participant uses their own streaming-service subscription and accesses content through the applicable provider.

The Watch Party room service does not relay DRM-protected video streams or streaming-account passwords to other participants.

Watch Party does not grant access to paid content without the appropriate subscription.

### Playback Synchronization

StreamNest attempts to coordinate playback between participants where supported by the streaming provider.

Some streaming services may restrict automatic synchronization or playback controls.

### Room Information

Room information is processed by the Watch Party service to support viewing sessions. Leaving a room does not necessarily result in the immediate deletion of all server-side information.

Users should avoid sharing sensitive personal information through participant names or room information.

## 6. Application Updates

The Microsoft Store edition of StreamNest uses Microsoft Store update services.

GitHub-distributed editions may communicate with GitHub to check for application updates.

These services may process network information according to their respective privacy policies.

## 7. Information Sharing

StreamNest uses third-party services to provide catalog discovery, streaming access, Watch Party coordination, and software distribution.

These services may include:

- Streaming providers for official streaming access.
- TMDB for movie and television catalog information.
- Cloudflare for catalog requests and Watch Party coordination.
- Microsoft Store or GitHub for software distribution and updates.

Information is processed by the relevant service operators when users access their services.

Obsidian Interactive does not sell personal information collected through StreamNest.

## 8. Data Storage and Security

StreamNest stores application preferences and browser-session data locally on the user's computer.

Network requests use HTTPS where supported. The shared TMDB API credential is maintained as a Cloudflare secret rather than distributed with the application.

Watch Party coordination information is processed by a Cloudflare-hosted service.

Users should protect their Windows accounts because access to local application data may expose saved session information.

Third-party services maintain their own security practices and retention policies.

## 9. User Choices and Data Removal

Users can change selected providers, remove items from their watchlist, and sign out through the relevant streaming services.

Users may choose whether to participate in Watch Party sessions and may leave rooms when finished.

Locally stored application information and browser sessions may remain on the device until cleared. Uninstalling the application does not necessarily delete all saved application data or previously transmitted server-side information.

Users can request assistance with locating and clearing StreamNest's locally stored information through the support channel below.

## 10. Children's Privacy

StreamNest is not specifically designed for children. Streaming content accessed through the application may include mature themes and age-restricted programming.

Parents and guardians should use the parental controls provided by the relevant streaming services.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated as StreamNest's features, integrations, or data-handling practices change.

Updates will be published on the official StreamNest project page.
